The shack

Jacksonville's garlic crab tradition

Garlic crabs are a Jacksonville original with roots in Gullah Geechee cooking, and Zebo's serves them by the tray: crabs coated in seasoning and garlic, with sausage, corn, potatoes and garlic eggs on the side.

Not in a crab mood? Fried shrimp baskets and fish baskets come out hot too.
